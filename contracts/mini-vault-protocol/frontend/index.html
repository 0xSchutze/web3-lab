Protocol Details

Welcome to your personal on-chain treasury. Anyone can deposit ETH or whitelisted ERC20 tokens into this smart contract. Only the private key of the assigned owner can initiate withdrawals. Authorized addresses can manage the whitelist and emergency pause state.
